Who I am

First year at MIT Academy of Engineering, Alandi, Pune — Civil Engineering, batch of 2029. But honestly, engineering is just one part of what I do. The other part is building things with code, AI, and design — and that side never really stops.

I love figuring out how tools like Claude Code, Codex, and LeetCode-style problem solving work under the hood — not to become a software engineer, but because understanding how to build things with AI changes what's possible. I use these tools to create real stuff: apps, automations, portfolios, and workflows that actually solve problems.

Right now I'm deeply into AI-generated UGC and creatives — using AI to produce scroll-stopping content, ads, and visuals for brands and creators. It's where design instinct meets machine output, and getting that balance right is something I genuinely obsess over.

On the creative side, photo and video editing are at a professional level — Lightroom is second nature. I help people with their digital presence, and those small projects keep turning into bigger ones. The thread running through all of it is simple: I want to build things that work and look like someone actually cared.

Semester 01 — IoT
Smoke Detector System
Group project · Sensor threshold logic · IoT concepts

A system that detects smoke or pollution levels and triggers an alarm when readings cross a configurable threshold. The alert level is adjustable through code — making it an adaptable system rather than a fixed one. A simple idea that required real debugging to actually work.

Getting the sensor to respond correctly to actual smoke concentrations was harder than expected. That gap between how something should work and how it actually behaves is where the real learning happens.

My Contributions

Understanding and documenting the full working concept
Presenting the project to faculty
Helping work through the coding logic
Team coordination across all build phases
Status: Completed  ·  Threshold: adjustable  ·  Semester 1
